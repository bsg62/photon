A local photo manager for everyone who misses Picasa

photon is a free Picasa alternative for Linux, macOS and Windows. It shows the photos in your folders and leaves the files as they are.

Download Other systems

MIT licensed · No account or internet connection needed

Your files stay as they are

photon does not move, rename or change photos. Albums, crops and names are kept in its own library on your computer.

Fast

photon uses your graphics card to draw, so even very large libraries scroll smoothly.

Reads Picasa's data

The stars, people, albums and hidden photos you set in Picasa show up in photon, with nothing to export.

Features

Search

Search file and folder names, camera, lens, keywords, captions and dates, and narrow the results with terms. The ? in the box lists every term, and a search can be saved.

The viewer

The info panel shows camera data, a histogram, dates, location, people, keywords and albums. Zoom in to full size, and rotate or crop without changing the file.

Duplicates and look-alikes

photon finds identical copies and resized or re-saved versions of a photo. Up to four photos can be compared side by side.

Reorganise in your file manager

Rename or move photos and folders; they keep their albums, keywords, crops and names.

People

Optional face detection groups faces on your computer. Name them and search by name.

Albums and keywords

Make albums, and browse the keywords Lightroom, Bridge or digiKam wrote into your photos.

Hide photos

Hide a photo or a folder with H. It leaves every view; the file is not touched.

Rotate, crop, export

Rotate and crop without changing the file, play a slideshow, and export copies at full size or smaller for email.

Fast wherever your photos are

Photos on an external drive or a network share browse as quickly as the ones on your computer.

Questions

Where does photon keep its library?

In a folder called io.github.bsg62.photon in your user data directory, and its thumbnails in your cache directory. Where that is on each system.

What should I back up?

Your photos, as always, and photon's library folder, copied while photon is closed. It holds what exists only in photon: albums, keywords you added, rotations and crops, names and settings.

What happens when I rename or move photos?

Inside the folders photon watches, a renamed or moved photo or folder keeps its albums, keywords, rotations and crops, and the names on its faces. What is and isn't followed.

Can I keep using Picasa alongside it?

Yes. Both read and write the same files, and photon picks up a change Picasa makes within a few seconds.
